Getting around

Kennedy Station · the TTC, roads and traffic
Running the TTC costs $3.0 billiona year, 2026. The city itself pays $1.5B of it; fares and others pay the rest. That makes it the city's single biggest program. Roads, traffic signals and road safety are a separate budget: $516 millionTransportation Services, a year, 2026. Traffic is the top problem in two recent polls.
Who decides: city council sets the TTC's budget, fares and service levels each year.
